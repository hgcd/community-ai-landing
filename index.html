We build custom technology for small organizations.

For small businesses, nonprofits, and community groups, more of what once required a large technology team is becoming possible. We bring the expertise to help you work out what’s useful and make it work.

Why tailored technology is becoming more accessible

AI and other development tools are changing how software can be built and adapted. Some capabilities can now be explored with less time and effort, opening up options that smaller organizations might previously have ruled out.

That could mean an assistant that understands your services, a dashboard built around the information you need, or a tool that connects systems you already use. The aim is technology shaped around your work and resources.

Getting there still takes expertise: understanding the work, choosing the right approach, connecting tools safely, and planning for use and maintenance. That’s where Community AI comes in.

What we could build together

These examples show ways to add capabilities without replacing the tools you rely on. We can fill a gap, connect separate systems, or extend an existing website, database, or dashboard.

AI front desk for website or phone

Add answers and request routing to your website or phone, with a handover to your team when needed.

AI knowledge and research workspace and toolkit

Bring your sources together to find information and prepare source-linked research, summaries, and briefs.

Internal organization assistant

Help staff find answers across the policies, procedures, and shared knowledge you already have.

Custom website or mobile app

Add a tailored online service to your website, or create a companion mobile app for your audience.

Custom client, member, or participant portal

Give people a place to apply, register, book, and follow progress, connected to your existing work.

Digital service platform

Connect your program information, applications, communication, and follow-up in one experience.

Custom analytics and impact intelligence

Build a view of the measures, patterns, and outcomes that matter, using data from your current tools.

Comprehensive data management environment

Organize information across your existing sources with structures and tools shaped around your work.

Organization-wide data and reporting toolkit

Give staff a consistent way to collect, analyze, and report information from the tools they use.

Connected operations workspace

Bring key information and tasks from your existing systems into a shared view.

Workflow automation and systems integration

Connect the tools you already have to simplify repetitive entry, handoffs, and follow-up.
